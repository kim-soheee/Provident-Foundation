ABOUT US

Provident Foundation

We help individuals, financial advisors, businesses, churches, and schools to make a positive impact through our Donor Advised Funds.

These Donor Advised Funds financially support Canadian registered charities and other qualified donees.


Our Promise

At Provident Foundation, we believe every gift has the potential to make a difference. We help individuals, families, businesses, ministries, and charities give with purpose through thoughtful charitable planning and stewardship. By simplifying administration, working alongside trusted advisors, and providing flexible giving solutions, we help maximize the impact of your generosity while ensuring your giving reflects the values and causes that matter most to you.
